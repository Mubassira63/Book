
Chapter 3: Unraveling the Mystery

John sat down beside a campfire, the map spread before him. The markings on the map seemed to be written in an ancient script. He had been trying to decipher it for days but had no luck. Just as he was about to give up, a voice in the wind whispered the answer.

"The key lies beneath the moonlight," the voice said.

John looked up, and for the first time, the solution seemed clear. He would have to wait for the full moon to reveal the hidden path.
